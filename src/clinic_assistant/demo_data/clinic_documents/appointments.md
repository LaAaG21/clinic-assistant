# Appointments and cancellations

Synthetic demo document for the fictional Cedar Grove Clinic. Not real clinic policy.

Appointments are 30 minutes long. Patients can book through the clinic chat or by calling reception on 555-0100.

Same-day bookings require staff approval. The appointment is confirmed only after staff approve the request.

Appointment cancellations require staff approval. The appointment stays booked until staff approve the cancellation.

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment to check in at reception.
